Privacy Policy

Silent Moon for iPhone and iPad · Effective date: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Silent Moon app (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles your information. The app is provided by Tinh Ngo.

The short version: the app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you set in the app is stored only on your device. We do not receive it, and we cannot see it.

1. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves a small amount of information in its private storage on your device:

WhatWhy
The topics you choose during setupTo remember your setup choices
Your reminder time and the days of the week you pickedTo schedule your reminders
Sessions and tracks you mark as favoritesTo show your favorites and the “My” filter
Sessions and tracks you mark for downloadTo show their download status
Where you stopped in each track (playback position)So you can continue where you left off
App preferences: selected category filter, narrator voice, and whether you have finished setup or seen the Sleep introductionSo the app opens the way you left it

This information:

  • stays on your device. The app does not send it to us or to anyone else. The app does not connect to any server of ours;
  • is not linked to your identity. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account;
  • is deleted when you delete the app (see section 5).

If you back up your device with iCloud or with a computer, iOS may include this app data in that backup. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple’s terms; we have no access to them.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive, store or share any personal information. In particular, the app:

  • has no sign-in or account;
  • contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or social-media SDKs, and no other third-party code that collects data;
  • does not track you across apps or websites, and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • does not show ads;
  • does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or health data.

If you have turned on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. Apple controls this, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

3. Notifications (opt-in)

The app can remind you to meditate at the time and on the days you choose.

  • Reminders are local notifications: your device schedules and shows them. They are not sent from a server, and we do not receive a device token or any other identifier.
  • The app asks for notification permission only when you tap SAVE on the Reminders screen. If you tap NO THANKS, the app does not ask and does not schedule anything.
  • You can turn notifications off at any time in Settings › Notifications › Silent Moon. The rest of the app works the same with notifications on or off.

4. Advertising and tracking

There is no advertising and no tracking in the app. We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect any.

5. Deleting your data

Because all app data is kept on your device, you are in full control of it:

  • Delete the app (touch and hold the app icon › Remove App › Delete App). iOS removes all data the app stored and every reminder it scheduled.
  • “Offload App” keeps the app’s data on your device. To erase it, use Delete App.

We do not hold a copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is a general-audience wellness app. It is not directed to children under 13, and it is not in the App Store Kids category. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information that a company holds about you. We do not hold any personal information collected through the app. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and we delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed.

8. Health information

The app offers general wellness content such as guided meditation and sleep music. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. The app does not collect health data.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled (for example by adding optional accounts), we will update this policy and the effective date before that version is released, and we will describe the change in the app’s release notes.
