Getting started

Do I need an account?

No. Silent Moon has no sign-in. Open the app, go through the short setup (choose a topic, set a reminder or skip it) and you’re ready.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. In version 1.0, all sessions and sleep tracks are included in the app, so you can use it without an internet connection.

Is my data stored online?

No. Your topics, reminder schedule, favorites and listening progress are saved only on your device. See our Privacy Policy.

Reminders and notifications

How do reminders work?

During setup, the Reminders screen asks what time and on which days you’d like to meditate.

  1. Pick a time and tap the days you want (at least one day; SAVE stays dimmed until you choose one).
  2. Tap SAVE. iOS asks if Silent Moon may send notifications. Tap Allow.
  3. You’ll get a “Time to meditate” notification at that time on each selected day, every week.

Tap NO THANKS if you don’t want reminders. The app won’t ask for permission and won’t schedule anything. Reminders are created on your device; they work without an internet connection.

I’m not getting reminders
  1. Open the iPhone or iPad Settings app.
  2. Tap Notifications, then Silent Moon.
  3. Turn on Allow Notifications. Make sure Lock Screen, Notification Center or Banners is selected.
  4. If a Focus mode (for example Sleep or Do Not Disturb) is on at your reminder time, it may silence the notification. Check Settings › Focus.

If you tapped Don’t Allow earlier, the app shows a Notifications are off message with an Open Settings button that takes you straight to step 3.

How do I stop reminders?

Go to Settings › Notifications › Silent Moon and turn off Allow Notifications. You can turn them back on at any time.

How do I change my reminder time or days?

In version 1.0, the reminder schedule is set during setup. To pick a new time or new days, reset the app (see below) and go through setup again. A reminder setting inside the app is planned for a future update.

Resetting the app

How do I reset Silent Moon or erase my data?

All your data is stored only on your device, so deleting the app erases it:

  1. Touch and hold the Silent Moon icon on the Home Screen.
  2. Tap Remove App, then Delete App, then Delete.
  3. Reinstall Silent Moon from the App Store. The app starts fresh with the setup screens.

This removes your favorites, listening progress, topics and all scheduled reminders.

Note: Offload App (in Settings › General › iPhone Storage) keeps your data. Use Delete App to erase it.

Will my favorites move to my new iPhone?

Silent Moon has no account and does not sync. If you restore your new device from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one, iOS may restore the app’s data along with it.
